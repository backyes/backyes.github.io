EPD Disaggregation: Elastic Encoder Scaling for Vision-Language Models in SGLang
rednote hilab, Alibaba Cloud Computing, AntGroup SCT
January 12, 2026

TL;DR:
- Encoder-Prefill-Decode (EPD) separates vision encoding from language processing
- Independent scaling of vision encoding capacity
- Compatible with PD disaggregation (three-tier)
- Flexible transfer backends: ZMQ, Mooncake
- Vision embedding caching
- 6-8x lower TTFT vs colocate at 1 QPS for image-heavy workloads
- For image-light scenarios: may be counterproductive (network latency overhead)

VIT SCALING PROBLEM:
- ViT does NOT benefit from increased TP (communication dominates)
- H20 + Qwen2.5-VL-72B benchmark (4 images/request):
  tp=2: 492ms, tp=4: 466ms, tp=8: 524ms
- Vision encoder has far fewer parameters than LLM

ARCHITECTURE:
1. Encoder Server (--encoder-only): vision-only, prefix multimodal cache, scale out
2. Prefill Server (--language-only): receives embeddings; with PD ships KV to Decode
3. Decode Server: standard decode-only, receives KV cache from prefill

KEY COMPONENTS:
- Image Distribution: data parallelism across encoder instances (not TP)
- Transfer Backends: zmq_to_scheduler (default), zmq_to_tokenizer, mooncake
- Vision Embedding Cache: default 4GB via SGLANG_VLM_CACHE_SIZE_MB

BENCHMARK:
- 8× H20 96GB GPUs, Qwen3-VL-235B-A22B-Instruct-FP8
- Text tokens: 128/256; Images/request: 1-8 (avg ~4); 1080p; QPS 0.2-1.0
- Colocate: 1 PD instance TP=4, 4 GPUs
- 1E1P: 1 encoder TP=1 + 1 PD TP=4, 5 GPUs
- 2E1P: 2 encoders TP=1 + 1 PD TP=4, 6 GPUs
- TTFT: 6-8x lower than colocate at 1 QPS
- TPOT: 8-10x lower
- Throughput: ~2x at 0.8-1.0 QPS
- Higher TPOT in 2e1p due to larger batch size during decoding

LIMITATIONS OF PD DISAGGREGATION (from this blog):
- Dedicated encoder GPUs may have lower utilization in image-light scenarios
- Additional network latency for embedding transfer
