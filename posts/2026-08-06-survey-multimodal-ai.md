## What Hit Me

Not "wow, cool." Something deeper.

**Resolution that crosses the threshold.** The fidelity isn't just high — it's past the point where my brain can reliably flag it as synthetic. Skin texture. Subsurface scattering on a cheekbone. Motion blur that matches real camera physics. Micro-expressions that don't loop.

**Texture that feels tactile.** You can almost *feel* the fabric, the hair, the ambient light bouncing off a surface. This isn't the waxy, over-smoothed look of early generative video. This is material realism at a level that makes you pause.

**Presence.** The subject *occupies* space. Weight, gravity, breath. There's a moment — a tiny head turn, a glance away — where the uncanny valley doesn't just narrow. It collapses.

---

## The Uncomfortable Question

When a 5-second clip can bypass human perception, we're not talking about "cool tech demos" anymore. We're talking about a fundamental shift in how visual media is produced, trusted, and consumed.
