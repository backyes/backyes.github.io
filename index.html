      When Do We Need a Global Address Service? — Beyond Programming Ease

      Unified addressing is often discussed as a programming-ease feature. But the real reason we need an independent, global address service is deeper: it is about amortizing the cost of establishing communication pipes, enabling data services at every latency tier to exchange data with lower overhead. From Redis to NVLink, the history of AI distributed systems is a history of address services evolving toward lower latency.
